## **Related Courses**

[← Back to Home](README.md)

#### **ECE 109 Introduction to Computer Systems**
Introduction to key concepts in computer systems. Number representations, switching circuits, logic design, microprocessor design, assembly language programming, input/output, interrupts and traps.

#### **ECE 200 Introduction to Signals, Circuits and Systems**
Introduction to circuit laws and common electronic components, basic transient and steady-state circuit behavior, signal analysis in time and frequency domains, analog processing with op‑amps, and weekly practical laboratory experience with test equipment.

#### **ECE 209 Computer Systems Programming**
Computer systems programming using the C language and its translation to Assembly.

#### **ECE 211 Electric Circuits**
Fundamentals of circuit theory and design (voltage, current, power, energy, basic passive elements), circuit analysis techniques (Kirchhoff’s laws, node/mesh, Thevenin/Norton), steady‑state and transient behavior, AC/DC and phasor methods, operational amplifiers, and transfer‑function analysis.

#### **ECE 212 Fundamentals of Logic Design**
Developed a state machine–based circuit using a 555 timer, D flip-flops, and integrated circuit chips to implement sequential LED turn signals in a model car. Designed and optimized the system with Boolean simplification and K-Maps, using Verilog to develop testbenches to validate circuit integrity; integrated the circuit into a 1:24 car for a functional prototype.

#### **ECE 301 Linear Systems**
Representation and analysis of linear systems using differential equations: impulse response and convolution, Fourier series, and Fourier and Laplace transformations for discrete time and continuous time signals. Emphasis on interpreting system descriptions in terms of transient and steady-state response. Digital signal processing.

#### **ECE 302 Microelectronics**
Addresses semiconductor device physics (diodes, BJTs, MOSFETs), device characteristics and circuit modeling, diode and transistor amplifier design and biasing, small‑signal analysis, multistage amplifiers, voltage regulation, and basic logic inverters.

#### **ECE 306 Introduction to Embedded Systems**
Introduction to designing microcontroller-based embedded computer systems using assembly and C programs to control input/output peripherals. Use of embedded operating system.

#### **ECE 308 Elements of Control Systems**
Analog system dynamics, open and closed loop control, block diagrams and signal flow graphs, input-output relationships, stability analyses using Routh-Hurwitz, root-locus and Nyquist, time and frequency domain analysis and design of analog control systems. Use of computer-aided analysis and design tools.

#### **ECE 309 Data Structures and Object-Oriented Programming for Electrical and Computer Engineers**
Advanced programming focusing on object-oriented design (C++). Object-oriented programming topics, classes, inheritance, polymorphism, abstract types, and generic types are described and applied to program design.

#### **ENG 331 Communication for Engineering and Technology**
Written communication in industrial and technical organizations, emphasizing internal communication with managers and technical personnel and including external communication with regulators, vendors, and clients. Intensive practice in writing; relationship of writing to oral and visual communication. 

#### **ECE 407 Introduction to Computer Networking**
Focuses on engineering principles of computer communications and networking, including layering concepts, overview of protocols, architectures for local, metropolitan, and wide-area networks, routing protocols, internet operations, transport control and applications, emerging issues in computer networks.

#### **ECE 484 ECE Senior Design I**
Applications of engineering and basic sciences to the total design of electrical and/or computer engineering circuits and systems. Consideration of the design process including concept and feasibility study, systems design, detailed design, project management, cost-effectiveness, along with development and evaluation of a prototype accomplished through design-team project activity. Supported with an introduction of key factors impacting the engineering design process including industrial design, finance, operations, etc. 

#### **ECE 492 Special Topics in ECE - Introduction to Robotics & Autonomous Systems**
Robotic fundamentals (dynamics, feedback control, motion planning, trajectory optimization, state estimation, localization, perception, robot learning) and system-level understanding of modern autonomous systems ranging from self-driving cars to humanoid robots. 

#### **ECE 492 Special Topics in ECE - Circuit Board Layout (Embedded Systems Hardware Design)**
Embedded hardware design and PCB development: foundations of embedded systems and schematic capture, PCB layout using KiCad (schematic, footprints, PCB routing, multilayer boards, power/ground planes, trace sizing and vias), component selection and ratings, power sources and interconnects, simulation and testing practices (Spice, test points/elements), mechanical and non‑circuit elements, fabrication considerations and regulatory/agency approvals.

#### **PY 205 Physics for Engineers and Scientists I**
Fundamental principles of mechanics of momentum, energy, and angular momentum; and oscillations and heat transfer phenomena.

#### **PY 206 Physics for Engineers and Scientists I Laboratory**
Lab course to accompany the PY 205 lecture course. A calculus-based study of mechanics, sound and heat.

#### **PY 208 Physics for Engineers and Scientists II**
Fundamental aspects of electricity and magnetism and their effects in materials

#### **PY 209 Physics for Engineers and Scientists II Laboratory**
Lab to accompany the PY 208 lecture course. A calculus-based study of electricity, magnetism, optics and modern physics.
